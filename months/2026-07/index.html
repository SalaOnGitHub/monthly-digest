Cerebras Builds a Cited Answer Engine for Enterprise Knowledge

On July 15, Cerebras detailed an internal knowledge base that handles 15,000 employee questions each day across Slack, wikis, code, and incident records. Its pipeline uses 3,072-dimensional pgvector HNSW search, builds six parallel retrieval lists, then applies reciprocal rank fusion and LLM reranking to return answers with citations.

Cerebras Puts Four Trillion Transistors on One Wafer

Cerebras presents the WSE-3 as the world's largest AI processor, with a 46,225 square millimeter die carrying 4 trillion transistors, 900,000 AI cores, and 125 petaflops. The company claims 19 times more transistors and 28 times more compute than NVIDIA B200; those comparisons are Cerebras figures based on its testing and can vary by workload and configuration.

3 of 10

Moonshot Takes Kimi K3 to 2.8 Trillion Parameters

Moonshot introduced Kimi K3 on July 17 as a 2.8-trillion-parameter model with native vision and a 1-million-token context window. Moonshot says its evaluations put K3 near the proprietary frontier while still trailing the leading systems overall, and it has promised full model weights on July 27.

Alibaba Previews the 2.4-Trillion-Parameter Qwen3.8

Alibaba previewed Qwen3.8-Max on July 19, describing a continuously evolving 2.4-trillion-parameter model available through its Token Plan, Qoder, and QoderWork. Alibaba calls it second only to Fable 5 and says open weights are coming soon, but independent testing must wait for a full release.

OpenAI Splits GPT-5.6 Into Sol, Terra, and Luna

On July 9, OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 in Sol, Terra, and Luna variants across ChatGPT, Codex, and its API. The company says Sol uses 54% fewer tokens for coding tasks and calls 5.6 its strongest cybersecurity model yet, with defensive uses including threat modeling, code review, patching, and blue teaming.

6 of 10

AMD Brings Microsoft Aboard Its First Rack-Scale AI System

AMD says Helios, its first rack-scale AI system, will ship later in 2026, with Microsoft joining Meta, OpenAI, and Oracle as an early customer. Each rack combines 18 compute trays, each carrying four Instinct GPUs and one EPYC CPU, as part of a full AMD stack spanning GPUs, CPUs, networking, and software.

7 of 10

Europe Orders Google to Open Android and Search to Rivals

On July 16, European Union regulators ordered Google to give rival AI assistants comparable Android access and competing search and AI services access to certain Google Search data. Search sharing must begin by January 2027 and Android changes by July 2027, with DMA noncompliance carrying fines up to 10% of annual worldwide turnover.

8 of 10

Apple Intelligence Clears a Regulatory Hurdle in China

Chinese regulators have approved Apple Intelligence for launch in China, clearing a key barrier to a rollout in Apple's major smartphone market. The arrangement uses Alibaba's Qwen as the primary model partner, with Baidu also involved, although approval makes deployment possible rather than setting a public launch date.

9 of 10

NVIDIA Opens Nemotron 3 Embed for Production Retrieval

On July 16, NVIDIA released open 8B and 1B Nemotron 3 Embed models for multilingual, code, and long-document retrieval with a 32,000-token context window. At publication the 8B model scored 78.5% and ranked first on RTEB, while NVIDIA says its NVFP4 1B variant reaches up to twice BF16 throughput on Blackwell and retains more than 99% of BF16 retrieval accuracy.

Anthropic and Blackstone Bet on AI Implementation

On July 15, TechCrunch detailed Ode with Anthropic, a $1.5 billion implementation joint venture backed by Anthropic, Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, Goldman Sachs, and others. Its 100 engineers build tailored enterprise systems under a Claude-first but not Claude-only approach, showing how forward-deployed implementation is becoming a competitive layer beyond model quality.
